Good questions, clear answers

Explore the details.

Does AIDoser require an automatic tester?

No. Enter your test results manually, or use supported connected sources such as Apex / Trident. Available integrations depend on your setup.

Which supplements can I use?

Configure channels for kalkwasser, sodium bicarbonate, calcium chloride, magnesium, sodium hydroxide, two-part solutions and other supported supplement strategies. Check compatibility, concentration and tubing requirements for each chemical.

Are the graphs live aquarium data?

No. The interactive graph on this marketing page is illustrative. The production dashboard requires a connected AIDoser and configured data sources.

Can I customize dosing schedules and alerts?

The system supports configurable pump schedules, lighting-aware dosing, safety thresholds and notifications. Available options depend on the installed firmware and connected equipment.
