Connect AIDoser to Wi-Fi

Keep this page open while you power the device and connect it to your home network.

1
Power on the AIDoserConnect the power supply and wait approximately 30 seconds for the controller to start.
2
Open Wi-Fi settings on your phone or computerLook for the temporary AIDoser setup network broadcast by the device.
3
Join the AIDoser setup network After connecting, the Wi-Fi setup page should open automatically so you can select your home Wi-Fi and enter its password.
4
If the setup page does not open automatically Stay connected to the AIDoser hotspot, open a web browser, and go directly to http://192.168.4.1. Do not reconnect to your home Wi-Fi until the AIDoser network setup is complete.
5
Return to this wizardThe next step will automatically detect when your AIDoser appears online.
IMPORTANT: If the connection page does not appear, stay connected to the AIDoser hotspot and open http://192.168.4.1 in your browser.

After Wi-Fi connects, write down the IP address shown by the AIDoser. You will need that address to open the local device setup page.
Do not connect dosing tubing or chemicals yet. We will verify the device and calibrate the pumps before dosing is enabled.
